Portfolio — reimagined

Organized by venue, matching your live build: Hyperliquid · Polymarket · Robinhood tabs with open-position counts, and Open / Closed tabs inside each venue. Rows stay compact snapshots; tapping one slides the 65% bottom sheet with the chart (entry marked) and full detail. Everything is clickable in the mockup.

1 · One money row, three levels

Portfolio, venue, and position all report through the identical labeled Value · P&L $ · P&L % row. The venue rollup + its Available Balance sit at the top of each Open tab, so "what do I have here and what's it doing" is answered before the first row.

2 · Venue tabs, like your build

Venue-first matches how the money actually works — balances, fees, and settlement live per venue — and the count badge on each tab tells you where your open risk is before you switch. Robinhood shows its USDG balance; the others show USDC.

3 · Open vs Closed

Open rows are the live snapshot (identity + risk one-liner + value + P&L pill). Closed rows are receipts: ticker, close side · size @ price · date, realized P&L, and the explorer link. No chevron — they open the explorer, not the sheet.

4 · The sheet — chart first

Tapping an open row slides the bottom sheet: 1D price (or odds) with a dashed line at your entry and a labeled dot at the moment you entered, then basis row (Entry · Mark · Invested / Bet · To Win · Shares / Avg Cost for spot), performance row (Value · P&L · P&L %), the liq or odds meter (perps/predictions only — spot has none), venue + date, and Close / Cash out / Sell. Actions live only in the sheet.

5 · Dimensions covered

All positions: entry, mark, $ invested, value, P&L $, P&L %. Perps add leverage, liq price, % to liquidation. Predictions add odds (entry → now), bet, to win, shares. Spot adds avg cost and share count. Venue level adds available balance per venue; portfolio level adds invested + total available.
